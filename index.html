About Francis

A family-owned business that protects your family

Since 1994, Francis Animal and Pest Control has provided services throughout Minnesota, Wisconsin, and Iowa. We are a family-owned and environmentally friendly business.

We always use live traps on animals to prevent any further distress and utilize precision placement of pesticides to ensure the safety of your home or business. We pride ourselves on completing every job with professionalism and effectiveness — we will not only solve the problem once, but also prevent it from happening again.

  • Family-owned and environmentally friendly
  • Live traps on animals — safe and humane
  • Precision placement of pesticides
  • Affiliated with Angie’s List and the Better Business Bureau
